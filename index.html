About Korra

Korra plans and hosts food experiences built around a menu, a story, and a room full of people — private dinners, brand pop-ups, workshops, and everything a host needs to bring a menu to life.

Every booking starts with a conversation about the occasion, the guests, and the kind of food that should be on the table — then Korra builds the menu, the format, and the styling around it.

The goal is never just a meal — it's an experience people remember and want to repeat.
  • Custom menusBuilt for the occasion, not picked off a fixed list.
  • End-to-end hostingMenu, styling, and service handled from first call to last plate.
  • Brand-readyExperience working white-label and in collaboration with other brands.
  • Odisha & BengalBased across both regions, with events hosted on-site or at your venue.
