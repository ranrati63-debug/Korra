What We Do

Eight ways to work with Korra — pick what fits, or ask us to blend a few together.

  1. Shopper Club

    A private, curated course of menus presented art-style — an intimate tasting experience.

  2. Brand Collaboration — Pop-ups

    Pop-ups built with other brands — co-branded menus, spaces & moments.

  3. Workshop

    Hands-on sessions where guests cook alongside us, built around a theme or technique.

  4. Basic Cooking

    Foundational cooking classes for anyone starting out in the kitchen.

  5. Buffet

    Themed and private buffets, fully customised to your event and guest list.

  6. Customising & Gifting

    Bespoke hampers, favours & gifting sets, styled and packed to your occasion.

  7. Food Events

    Larger gatherings that blend a workshop and a Shopper Club sitting into one experience.

  8. White Labeling

    Produce and package under your own brand — Korra handles the kitchen, you handle the label.
Our Story

About Korra

Korra brings food experiences across Odisha & Bengal — from private tastings to full-scale brand pop-ups.

Everything we do sits under two ideas: Education and Experience. We teach through workshops and basic cooking classes, and we create moments through Shopper Club sittings, themed buffets, and brand collaborations.
